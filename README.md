# My Favorite Series  
**Title:** Shangri-La Frontier  

*Shangri-La Frontier* is my favorite series because it shows how gaming can be more than just playing. It’s about challenge, fun, and friendship inside a virtual world.  

## Main Characters  
- Sunraku (Rakuro Hizutome)  
- Psyger-0 (Rei Saiga)  
- Arthur Pencilgon  
- Oikatzo  

## What I Like About It  
- The main character never gives up on hard games  
- The story is creative and full of action  
- The designs and fights look cool  

> "There’s no such thing as a bad game if you can enjoy it." – Sunraku
